Rysen HQ

← /work

Central operations, procurement and spend platform for an edtech group

Rysen HQ is a single, unified platform that will replace today's scattered tools: WhatsApp approvals, Zoho Expense, email-based purchase requests, Excel MIS, paper petty cash and manual asset tracking. It brings them into one structured system that founders can see and trust end to end. One place for approvals, spend, HR and operations, instead of HRMS, Odoo, Zoho and WhatsApp running side by side.

Planned the product brief (32 modules across three phases, used for sign-off) and led the verification and handover. The code lives in the company's private GitHub, so there is no public repository.

Features

Foundation

Identity and access with MFA, org and school setup, cost centres and budgets, category, item, vendor and service masters, approval engine

Request to receipt

Purchase requests, RFQ and quote comparison, purchase orders, goods receipts, service completion

Bills and payments

Bill capture and 3-way match, reimbursements (replacing Zoho Expense), petty cash, payments and bank files, vendor ledger

Operations

Tasks, SOPs and checklists, incidents, events, inventory and assets

Intelligence

Founder cockpit, auto-MIS and digests, anomaly engine

Next phase

Recruitment, attendance and leave, payroll, new-school opening playbook, vendor and auditor portals
