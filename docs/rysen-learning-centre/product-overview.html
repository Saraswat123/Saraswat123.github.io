What is Rysen Learning Centre?

One page, two readings: skip to For everyone if you just want to know what the product does. Skip to For engineers if you want the one-paragraph technical summary before diving into TECH_STACK.md / HLD.md / LLD.md.

For everyone

RYSEN Group of Schools runs 15 campuses and 50+ teachers. Before this system, that meant tracking who'd finished their training, who needed a password reset, which student passed which test, and who's owed a certificate — across WhatsApp groups, spreadsheets, and in-person check-ins. Rysen Learning Centre replaces all of that with one web app, with a different view for each kind of person who uses it:

  • A student logs in with their name and class (a teacher can also set them a short PIN), takes a timed multiple-choice test, and sees their score immediately. A leaderboard shows how their class is doing.
  • A teacher (educator) completes their own onboarding training, creates tests for their students, tracks who's falling behind, chats with other teachers in their group, and gets assigned tasks by the office with deadlines and a comment thread.
  • A campus head (principal) sees how their own campus's teachers are progressing through training, and does the same training themselves.
  • An admin (head office) manages every campus, every teacher, every student, every test, and every task from one dashboard — including a monthly "star rating" recognition program and a live Google Sheet that always shows the current roster and test results without anyone copy-pasting anything.

The one feature that saves the most time: a teacher or student finishing a stage of training or a programme gets their certificate the moment they pass — no admin has to notice, generate it, or send it. The system notices.

What it is not: it doesn't teach the material itself (that still happens in the classroom); it tracks who finished what, grades objective tests, and moves information so nobody has to chase it by hand.

For engineers

A Next.js 16 (App Router) app on Vercel, with Prisma 7 over a pooled connection to Neon serverless Postgres; four role-scoped portals (admin, educator, principal, student) share one API surface under app/api/, backed by cookie sessions with no JWT (every request hits the Session table). Email (Brevo/Resend), WhatsApp (Twilio), file storage (Vercel Blob), AI (Groq), and a live Google Sheet (OAuth2, no service account) are the external integrations; two Vercel Cron jobs do the only scheduled work. Full detail in TECH_STACK.md, HLD.md, and LLD.md; role-by-role click paths in USER_FLOWS.md.
